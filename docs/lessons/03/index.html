03 · Text Input

Reading the keyboard: scancodes, getchar, read_line.

What this lesson adds

The OS can now read the keyboard. It polls the hardware port for scancodes (the numbers a keyboard actually sends), turns them into characters with a lookup table, and builds getchar and read_line. The demo asks for your name and greets you back.

Boot it in your browser

Run the real LeveretOS lesson-3 ISO, emulated with v86.

This lesson is interactive , once it boots, click inside the screen and type your name, then press Enter.

Emulated in your browser, nothing is installed. The emulator + ISO take a few moments to load.
