This lesson is a reward: a playable Rock Paper Scissors game in the terminal. We will be adding no new OS systems, just combining everything built so far: 
read_line for input, strcmp to understand the player's choice, a simple counter for the computer's "random" move (% 3), and print/print_int for output.

Since we have no real random number source yet, the computer's move comes from a counter that changes each round, good enough randomness for a game.
